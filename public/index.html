03 / THE DIRECTION

BUILT FOR WORK
THAT COMPOUNDS.

Developer Studio Tampa is the independent practice of Theodore Castro, a Tampa-based photographer drawn to machines, spaces, people, and the moments that reveal what they are really worth.

Clients work directly with the person planning, shooting, editing, and delivering the work. Every project begins with where the images need to live and what they need to communicate.

Client production is the first engine: useful images, clear delivery, and repeatable relationships. The long-term aim is larger: original projects, stronger production capability, and eventually owned spaces and assets that give ambitious work room to happen in Tampa.

Available for one-time commissions, ongoing visual partnerships, and serious conversations about the studio's long-term direction.
