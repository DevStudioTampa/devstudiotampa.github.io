# Product decisions — v0.1

## Assumptions

- The first useful outcome is evidence that one clearly scoped offer earns qualified replies, quotes, and bookings within 30 days—not vanity activity.
- The owner works alone, on one primary desktop browser, and will manually collect public business facts and communicate through existing channels.
- Starter prices are editable hypotheses. They are not based on the prior TME project, claims about Tampa market rates, or promises of results.
- Demo organizations and people are explicitly fictional and use `example.com`; they demonstrate workflow only.

## Boundaries

- Data remains in browser storage. There is no backend, telemetry, account, sync, external login, inbox connection, Instagram scraping, automatic message sending, invoice integration, signature flow, or payment processing.
- “Suggestion” means transparent string and scoring rules. No neural network is trained or required.
- Every outreach draft is grounded only in pasted facts, editable, labeled for review, and copied manually.
- Funnel metrics count stage progression, not business outcomes beyond what the owner records. Small-sample rates are labeled directional.
- The quote is a printable scope proposal. Acceptance and money movement happen elsewhere.

## Safest next phase

Run the active Automotive Photo Session offer for 30 days with a small, relevant cohort. Review which segments reply, which objections recur, and whether at least one channel produces repeatable qualified conversations. Improve positioning from that evidence before adding automation.

Next engineering steps should be encrypted optional file persistence, robust CSV mapping with a preview/deduplication step, stronger quote numbering, and accessibility testing. Only after real message volume justifies it should an optional server-side AI suggestion adapter be considered. It must minimize shared data, disclose generated text, preserve the local provider, log approvals, and never send or change records without explicit human confirmation.
