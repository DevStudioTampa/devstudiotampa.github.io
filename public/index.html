03 / ABOUT

Built for
what comes next.

Founded by Theodore Castro in Tampa, Florida, Developer Studio Tampa is an independent company building creative work, digital products, and businesses with lasting value.

Photography and event coverage are its creative arm, with a focus on automotive, people, and the spaces they bring to life.

Alongside client work, DST is developing its own digital tools and platforms that solve everyday problems and help people make more informed decisions.

The goal is to grow a family of businesses under one parent company, built for long-term ownership and a legacy that can carry forward for generations.

Available for ongoing production partnerships and one-time projects.
