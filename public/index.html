03 / THE DIRECTION

BUILT FOR WORK
THAT COMPOUNDS.

Developer Studio Tampa is the independent practice of Theodore Castro, a Tampa-based photographer drawn to machines, spaces, people, and the moments that reveal what they are really worth.

Every project begins by defining what matters, who needs to see it, where the work will live, and how it should feel. That vision guides the work from planning through final delivery.

Today, that means commissioned photography and short-form motion delivered with clarity and consistency. Each project strengthens the relationships, capability, and financial base for larger original productions and, over time, owned creative spaces and assets in Tampa.

Available for one-time commissions and ongoing visual partnerships.
