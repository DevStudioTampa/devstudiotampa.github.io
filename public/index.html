03 / THE STUDIO

THEODORE CASTRO
TAMPA, FLORIDA.

Developer Studio Tampa was founded by Theodore Castro, a photographer and filmmaker based in Tampa, Florida.

He currently specializes in private cars and collections, garages, automotive events, exotic showrooms, and commercial work for brands and venues.

His style is built around controlled light, dark and rich tones, warm and teal color grades, reflections, motion blur, and sequenced frames that show more than one angle.

Theodore shoots on a Sony a7SII and uses a DJI RS3 for moving video. Photos are finished in Photoshop Express with custom Looks. Video is stabilized, color graded, and composited in DaVinci Resolve and Fusion.

Available for one-time bookings, commercial commissions, and ongoing partnerships.
