03 / THE DIRECTION

BUILT FOR WORK
THAT COMPOUNDS.

Developer Studio Tampa is Theodore Castro's independent image-making practice, focused on vehicles, spaces, events, and the people who bring them to life.

Every project starts with the same questions: What matters? Who needs to see it? Where will it live? How should it feel?

Today, the studio creates commissioned photography and short-form motion. The long view is larger original productions, a stronger creative network, and ownership of the spaces that make them possible.

Available for one-time commissions and ongoing visual partnerships.
